House style

Do's and don'ts

Everything below comes from watching what actually works at the bar. None of it is complicated, and none of it asks you to sound like an advert.

Do

  • Say you are an ambassador, in the same post, every time.
  • Name the lot you are drinking. People remember Guji, not "coffee".
  • Post the brew you actually made this morning, flaws and all.
  • Answer questions honestly, including when the answer is "I don't know".
  • Share the roast date. It is the most useful number on the bag.
  • Bring someone to the bar and let the coffee do the work.
  • Tell us when something tastes wrong. We would rather hear it from you.

Don't

  • Bury the disclosure in a reply, a bio, or the fourteenth hashtag.
  • Claim health benefits. Coffee is a drink, not a treatment.
  • Run paid ads on the Chowbeans name. That is our lane, not yours.
  • Post your link to coupon sites, deal forums or cashback aggregators.
  • Message strangers who did not ask. It costs us more than it earns.
  • Speak for the roastery. You speak for yourself, which is the point.
  • Recommend a lot you have never tasted, however good it sounds.

When you are not sure

The short test

Would it embarrass you?

If a friend seeing the post would think less of you for it, do not publish it.

Is it true this morning?

Tasting notes drift as a bag ages. Say what the cup is doing now, not what the label said.

Still unsure?

Ask in the community tab of the portal. Someone answers between pours.
