Ambassador program

Pour for the room.

Chowbeans grows by word of mouth and nothing else. Ambassadors share the drop, bring people to the bar, and earn beans for every cup that starts with them. Here is exactly how it works.

What you get

Four things

Beans on every referral

Anyone who orders through your link earns you beans. They keep earning after the first order.

First access to micro-lots

Small lots sell out in a morning. Ambassadors see them a day before the list goes public.

A seat at the cupping

We cup the new arrivals on Saturdays. Seats are limited and yours to claim with beans.

Gear from the shelf

Grinders, drippers, scales. The same equipment we use behind the bar, not branded merchandise.

How beans work

Earn

  • A friend orders with your link50 beans
  • You bring someone to the bar30 beans
  • You post a brew and tag us20 beans
  • You finish a challenge quiz20 beans

Spend

  • A bag from the current drop250 beans
  • A seat at a Saturday cupping400 beans
  • A hand grinder from the shelf900 beans

Who it is for

Eligibility

No follower count

We have never once looked at one. Bringing four people who stay beats four thousand who scroll past.

Anywhere we ship

If we can get a bag to you, you can be an ambassador. The bar is in Tokyo, the program is not.

Eighteen or older

You need to be eighteen to hold an account and to claim anything from it.

House rules

Short version

  • One account per person.
  • Only recommend what you have actually tasted.
  • No paid advertising against the Chowbeans name.
  • Beans expire twelve months after you earn them.
  • We close accounts that game the program. It has happened twice.

Getting in

Three steps

Apply

A short form. Contact details, a few questions about how you drink coffee.

Hear back within a week

A person reads every application. We roast on Tuesdays and review on Wednesdays.

Get your link

Issued the day you are approved, along with your first challenges.

Ready

Join the bar

Applications take about a minute. If you are already an ambassador, sign in to pick up your challenges and beans.
